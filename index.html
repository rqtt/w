— about me

About

i am a high school student on the math and sciences track, with a strong passion for coding, technology and personal development. i enjoy exploring the world of software development and digital innovation, as i believe coding is a powerful way to transform ideas into useful and meaningful projects.

studying math and sciences shapes the way i build things: breaking a big problem into small steps, testing ideas, and not stopping until i understand why something works. that's the part of programming i like most.

i like working on every layer of a project. on the low-level side, i write c++ and lua — like luabridge, a scripting bridge that lets c++ apps load and safely run plugins. on the web, i build full apps with next.js and typescript, like a dashboard for managing a game's admin settings and moderation. and with python i make data tools, like candle$$, which charts markets with indicators such as rsi and macd. this site is one of my ongoing projects too.

i also have a creative side. i use photoshop, after effects, canva and blender for visuals, and i'm learning fl studio and ableton live to make my own music. there's almost always something playing while i work — you can see what in the music tab.

i am actively involved in international and community-based experiences, including erasmus+ projects, which have helped me develop communication skills, cultural awareness, teamwork, and adaptability. i am also a member of the romanian rotary club, where i take part in activities focused on leadership, volunteering, and making a positive impact in the community.

my passions include learning new skills, working with people, discovering new cultures, creating projects, and constantly challenging myself to grow. if you want to see what i'm working towards right now, check out the quest log.
